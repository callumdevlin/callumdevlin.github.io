# RX-8 core pages: fact-check report

Date accessed: 2026-10-14. Scope: quick-reference.md, compression-testing.md, torquespecs.md, chassis.md, drivetrain.md, fuel-ignition-and-starting.md (all under ProjectPages/RX8/). Read-only check; nothing in the repository was edited.

## Summary: the ten most consequential findings

1. **Oil pressure (quick-reference):** page says 500 kPa at 3,000 rpm / 100 degC "high-power engine". The cited Series 1 manual gives ONE value, 350 kPa {3.57 kgf/cm2, 50.8 psi} at 3,000 rpm, 100 degC, with no standard/high-power split (engine spec page and the oil-pressure inspection page agree). 500 kPa also exceeds the oil-pump relief-valve opening pressure in the same table (441-490 kPa). DISCREPANCY. The only "500" I could find is a search-snippet of a 2009 service-highlights PDF I could not open.
2. **Pulley lock bolt (torquespecs):** page 300-340 Nm; manual 254-294 N.m {25.9-29.9 kgf.m}. DISCREPANCY (this is a safety-critical eccentric-shaft fastener).
3. **Thread condition on engine fasteners (torquespecs):** page says values assume clean dry threads and gives "Red Loctite" for the flywheel/counterweight locknut. Manual: locknut threads get silicone sealant (EC manual; the US-style FSM says sealant on the seating face), tension bolts get engine oil plus a new seal washer, pulley lock bolt gets engine oil plus new O-ring plus sealant on seating face. DISCREPANCY.
4. **Steering gear mounting bolts (torquespecs):** page "88" Nm single value; manual 74.4-104.8 N.m. The tie-rod nut rows also conflate fasteners: manual shows 37.0-49.0 N.m for the tie-rod end stud nut (rack R/I) and for the toe locknut (alignment page), but 68.6-98.0 N.m for the "Locknut" on the tie-rod end in the rack D/A figure; the page's "inner nut (rack end) 69-98" is not a fastener Mazda describes. DISCREPANCY/PARTIAL.
5. **Fuel pressure (quick-reference, fuel page):** "running" 375-450 kPa. Mazda's test is engine STOPPED, ignition ON, pump driven by grounding check-connector terminal F/P. No running-pressure figure exists on the cited pages. PARTIAL (condition wrong; numbers and psi conversions right).
6. **Brake fluid (chassis, quick-reference):** page says DOT 3 only. The manual's UK-market spec is "SAE J1703, FMVSS 116 DOT3 or DOT4" for brakes, same as clutch. The pages are also internally inconsistent (clutch line says "DOT 3 or DOT 4 for the UK specification"). DISCREPANCY.
7. **Differential oil and Super-LSD (quick-reference, drivetrain):** manual says API GL-5, SAE 90 only (1.2-1.4 L replacement; 1.3 L spec table). "80W-90 or 75W-90" is not in the manual. Torque-bias ratio 2.0 and initial torque 49 Nm are not in any page I could open (UNVERIFIED), and "not a clutch-plate unit/no friction modifier" is unsupported.
8. **Unverifiable torque rows (torquespecs):** "Oil pan upper block bolts 18.6-25.5" and "Rear stationary gear stopper screw 3.2-4.7 + thread-locker" do not appear in the engine-assembly pages (ENG E08-E10) or the oil-pan R/I page. "Four flange nuts (incl. at diff)" for the propshaft is not supported: the figure shows one bolt/nut group at the differential flange, 45-59 N.m. All wrench sizes (Size column) are not from Mazda; 17 mm for wheel nuts looks wrong for M12x1.5 (check physically).
9. **KEMSO retrofit (fuel page):** vendor numbers CONFIRMED (SKU 10910, 340 L/h at 43 psi 13.5 V, 43-90 psi, 3.8 cm body). But the install steps omit Mazda's requirement to drain the tank to half full or less before opening the pump (fuel above pump flange at 3/4 gauge), the SST lock-ring method (hand one full turn, then 50-140 deg and 80-135 N.m, total 410-500 deg) and Mazda's post-install leak test. Rotary Revs D585 page could not be opened (TLS certificate mismatch), so all D585/Magnecor/adaptor statements are UNVERIFIED.
10. **General:** many torque ranges are rounded outward to integers (e.g. spark plug 13-18 vs 12.8-17.7 given correctly elsewhere on the site; steering shaft nuts 16-23 vs 15.7-22.5; intake manifold 19-26 vs 18.6-25.5). The cited manual is the 2003-MY "EC" Series 1 edition (engine-folder Market.html = EC, ModelYr.html = 2003); a 2005 UK car may differ in running changes. charm.li was unreachable throughout (ECONNREFUSED); equivalent foxed.ca pages were used instead.

## Source conventions and method

- SRVC = `https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/` (page code follows, e.g. BHE010002000T04). ENG = `https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/engine/A03/html/`. FSM-03 / FSM-06 = `https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/Manuals/RX-8FSM/03EngineDetails.pdf` and `06DriveTrain.pdf` (a US-style Series 1 FSM; text extracted locally with pdftotext; used only as a cross-check and flagged where it differs).
- Manual identity: ENG/Market.html reads "Market: EC", ENG/ModelYr.html reads "Modelyr: 2003", ModelDesc "RX-8". SRVC pages say "European (L.H.D. U.K.) specs." and "Australian specs." where markets differ; the manual has some R.H.D. illustrations but no UK-specific RHD service data. SRVC Market.html/ModelYr.html return 404.
- Many Mazda torque values appear only in the figure PNGs (callouts), not in the page text. I opened those PNGs (images/CHU....png) and read the callouts; the figure code is quoted as "fig".
- Verdicts: CONFIRMED, DISCREPANCY, PARTIAL, UNVERIFIED, NOT-APPLICABLE-TO-VARIANT. "CONFIRMED (rounded)" means the figure matches but has been rounded outward past the manual's limit by 0.1-0.5; recommended wording keeps Mazda's decimals.
- Conversions recomputed (1 psi = 6.89476 kPa): 220 kPa = 2.20 bar = 31.9 psi; 830 kPa = 8.30 bar = 120.4 psi; 680 kPa = 6.80 bar = 98.6 psi; 150 kPa = 21.8 psi; 100 kPa = 14.5 psi; 375-450 kPa = 3.75-4.50 bar = 54.4-65.3 psi (manual prints 65.2); 200 kPa = 29.0 psi; 225/45R18 diameter = 2 x 101.25 + 457.2 = 659.7 mm. All page conversions are correct.

## Cross-page contradictions and owner's-choice statements stated as fact

- Spark-plug torque: 13-18 Nm (torquespecs) vs 12.8-17.7 Nm (quick-reference, compression, fuel). Mazda: 12.8-17.7.
- RE7C-L: "is fitted on this car" (quick-reference) vs "probable fitted set; confirm" (fuel). Not in Mazda's list.
- Brake fluid DOT 3 only (quick-reference, chassis) vs clutch "DOT 3 or DOT 4 for UK" (quick-reference). Mazda: both DOT3 or DOT4 for the European/UK spec.
- Tie-rod locknut: torquespecs/chassis use 37-49; torquespecs also lists 69-98 for an "inner nut"; Mazda's own pages disagree with each other (37.0-49.0 vs 68.6-98.0).
- Pulley bolt/flywheel nut "dry thread/Red Loctite" statements conflict with Mazda's oil/silicone-sealant instructions.
- Front disc 323 mm labelled "high-power" (chassis); Mazda keys it to sport vs standard suspension.
- Owner choices already flagged on-page and acceptable: 10W-40 oil; Michelin Pilot Sport 5; custom PA11 clip; Devlin Rotary tester. Owner choices NOT flagged: 98 octane, RE7C-L, RHD bleed order, wrench sizes, 220 kPa tyre pressure (placard).
- Variant notes: coolant capacity 9.8 L (EC/UK manual) vs 8.7 L (US-style FSM); plug gap 1.05-1.15 mm is the European (L.H.D. U.K.) figure (Australian 1.15-1.25); front brake discs 303 mm (standard) vs 323 mm (sport); FP2/RP2 injectors and auxiliary port are 13B-MSP High power only; single-cooler (5.8 L) vs twin-cooler (6.7 L) oil totals.
