# RX-8 S1 OMP pages: fact-check report

Date accessed (all sources): 2026-10-14. Read-only check; no repo file was touched.
Pages checked: ProjectPages/RX8/OMP/{index,how-it-works,testing,servicing,nozzles-premix,lab}.md, ProjectPages/RX8/sohn-adapter.md, ProjectPages/RX8/enginedata.md.

Method note: WebFetch summarises, so for the Mazda manual mirror and the RX8Club threads I also pulled the raw HTML with plain curl into the scratchpad and read the exact text/table structure. Where a source returned 403 / a bot challenge I did not work around it and marked the claim UNVERIFIED.

Abbreviation: F/ = https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/ (Mazda 2003 RX-8 workshop manual mirror). Mazda page codes: T07 = BHE011101008T07 (OMP construction/operation), T37 = BHE014000140T37 (block diagram), T38 = BHE014000140T38 (control operation), W01/W02/W03 = BHE010201600W01/02/03 (DTC P1686/7/8), W31 = BHE010300103W31 (engine control operation inspection), SW = BHE014014600W01 (OMP switch inspection), INS = BHE011114100W03 (OMP inspection), RI = BHE011114100W01 (OMP removal), NZ = BHE011114100W02 (nozzle removal), DT = BHE010200102W11 (DTC table), PID = BHE010200102T05, PCM = BHE014018880W02 (PCM inspection/terminal table).

## Summary

1. The core Mazda numbers in the pages are right: stepper logic (60-step initial set, 60-step monitor, "above step 52"), coil table and PCM terminals 2W/2AB/2V/2Y, 400 ohm switch limit, 30.6-35.6 ohm windings (D-B, D-F, C-A, C-E), 2N voltages, DTC titles/conditions, terminal assignments, nozzle vacuum figures (and their kPa/kgf/psi conversions), and the owner's-manual oil statements (found in Mazda USA's own 2004 PDF).
2. Real discrepancy (how-it-works.md): Mazda's fail-safe table gives one ignition-timing value, "Fixed at 4.88 deg CA", in a cell that spans both the Leading and Trailing rows. The page says "on the leading side" only. Also the fuel-injection fail-safe has an extra row ("At engine start: Regular control") that the page omits.
3. Owner-thread attributions that are stronger than the source: "~30 degrees the wrong way after removal or refit" (it was a deliberate full-range move for more oil, 2007, and 30 deg was an eyeball estimate; another poster estimated 19 deg and 10.5 deg); "trained technician"; "sump level can rise because oil gets past the rings" (not supported: one owner, replies blame fuel dilution); step ~21 is a "best guess"; 3,000-3,500 rpm: I could only confirm 3,000/3,300 (RX8Club, 2005).
4. Premix table arithmetic is correct to rounding except one cell: 50 L at the upper rate is 195.3 mL, the page shows 196. Ratios 1:512 / 1:256 and the UK club 1:488 / 1:203 conversions are correct.
5. renewablelube.com (page and PDF datasheet) could not be opened (Cloudflare challenge/403). Retailer copies of the product text: Mazdatrix gives 1/4-1/2 oz per gallon for non-modified NA daily-driver gasoline; the ARD NZ copy gives only 1 oz per 4 gal for that case. Higher rates are given for alcohol fuels / track / boosted. Check the live page.
6. UNVERIFIED (not opened): Engine Builder hose article and N3H1-13-221E, the 4206F letter text/scope, Mazda's own Series 2 document. Series 2 differences are supported by owner reports only.
7. enginedata.md: "nearly 3x" = 2.77x (OK), but "same idle" is wrong (1,290 vs 820 rpm); 1,000 and 6,000 rpm map rows are not in the listed steady-state holds; major method gaps (bench drive speed, per-outlet vs total ml/min, inlet pressure, calibration version) are listed under "questions only the author can answer".
8. Limit on all Mazda figures: the manual is the 2003 RX-8 manual; Mazda later issued PCM calibration changes (4206F in 2006, TSB 01-014/08 in 2008) that I cannot compare against, so fail-safe constants may differ on later software.
