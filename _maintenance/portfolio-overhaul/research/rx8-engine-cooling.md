# RX-8 engine rebuild / cooling / manual-fan-switch fact-check and hose research

Date accessed: 2026-10-14. Read-only exercise: no repository file was edited. Target car: 2005 UK RHD 231 PS six-port 13B-MSP, manual.

## How the evidence was gathered (reliability note)

- Engine manual: the Mazda "Engine Workshop Manual 13B-MSP (1773-1U-03C)", part no. 9999-95-E13B-MSP09, printed October 2008, revised 6/2008 and 9/2008 (the PDF at foxed.ca named in the brief, 71 pages). The tool saved the PDF and I extracted its text locally with pdftotext and read all of it directly. This is the highest-reliability source here. Section/page numbers below are the manual's own (e.g. 01-10-16).
- Mazda 2003 RX-8 web manual pages (foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE....html): fetched through WebFetch, which returns a model-summarised rendering, not raw HTML. I cross-checked key numbers across several pages (fan thresholds on three pages, cap pressure on two, thermostat on three, coolant procedure on its own page). One flow-chart image (BHE0112T001.png) was saved locally and viewed directly.
- Parts/forum/retailer pages: only pages that actually opened are cited as evidence. Anything that returned 403/404/redirect is listed at the end and any figure that only appeared in a search-result summary is marked UNVERIFIED.
- charm.li was not needed; the foxed.ca mirror covered the Mazda text.

## Summary (most consequential findings)

1. Engine page: the apex-groove statement is wrong in framing. Mazda's own revised manual prints 0.042-0.101 mm in the inspection procedure (01-10-16) but still prints 0.042-0.077 mm in its Technical Data table (01-50-1), with 0.15 mm max in both. It is an internal conflict in the same October 2008 manual, not "older vs revised".
2. Engine page: oil-pan content mixes engine "Types". Mazda's manual (00-00-12) says Type B applies only to US VIN ranges JM1 FE172/FE174/FE17M/FE17P *9# 400001 onwards (2009 MY); everything else, including a 2005 UK car, is Type A. Type A has one oil pan (8.8-11.8 N.m), no "upper pan"; "upper pan 18.6-25.5 N.m" exists only on Type B, whose lower pan is 7.8-10.8 N.m. The same mix is on the torque page.
3. Engine page: the reassembly order contradicts Mazda and itself. The lettered spacer is not fitted in the front-housing build (Mazda fits it in Housing Assembly II); end play is measured at step 8 before the front parts and pulley exist; rear housing is fitted before the regulator/stationary gear/oil seal; and the flywheel is fitted after the steps that need it locked.
4. Engine page: "Preparing for dismantling" says remove the flywheel first, but Mazda locks the shaft via the flywheel to remove the pulley lockbolt and oil-pump sprocket nut, and removes the flywheel only in Housing Disassembly III.
5. Engine page: end-play "maximum 0.10 mm" is not in Mazda's manual (standard 0.04-0.09 only). Spacer B = 8.005 mm is correct (A-E = 7.985/8.005/8.025/8.045/8.065).
6. ~120 kg: Mazda gives no engine mass in any manual I read. Uncited secondary figures: 112 kg (excludes airbox, alternator, starter, cover, fluids) and ~138 kg (304 lb, 2003 forum post attributing it to Mazda). UNVERIFIED as a Mazda figure.
7. Cooling page: all quick-reference numbers CONFIRMED (9.8 L; 80-84/95 C, lift >=8.5 mm; cap 73.3-103.3 kPa; fan 97/94 and 101/98 C; motor currents 8.9-11.9 A and 4.0-7.0 A). Gaps: Mazda also runs fans with the ignition OFF ("after-cooling", high speed) and the motor currents are a bench test, not an in-circuit reading. "No separate block drain" is true of Mazda's procedure only; a retailer lists a centre-iron coolant plug (9951-01-000) as fitting 2004-2011 13B-MSP. FL22 and "plastic plug" and "7-8 L" are not in Mazda's text.
8. Fan-switch page: circuit description is consistent with Mazda (relay No.1 = both fans low; Nos.1+2+3 = high; low-side drive on 5X/5AD; terminal E to PCM, terminal A from main relay). Main weaknesses: P0480/P0481 are a continuous monitor with a stated low-voltage trigger, so a pending code is expected rather than "potential"; relays 2 and 3 have separate terminal-E wires joined at 5AD, so the added ground must go on the common 5AD wire; after-cooling not mentioned.
9. Hose register: H01-H11 is the site's own labelling; Mazda's manual has no hose-by-hose list or part numbers. Catalogue evidence supports the H01-H08 part families; H07/H08 feed/return directions look reversed; "front/rear metal joining pipe" is one pipe (N3H2-15-190); H09-H11 UK RHD numbers: no evidence found (FE01-61-211/-213/-212D are flagged "won't fit RHD" by Atkins Rotary).
10. Hose dimensions with real sources: only Dayco 72615 (lower: ID 34.9 mm, OD 45.2 mm), Dayco 71766 (upper: IDs 31.75 / 34.9 mm), Gates 23059 (ID 33 mm, centreline 295 mm) and a few used-hose forum measurements (44 mm and 46 mm OD on a UK 231). Retailer cross-references conflict, so treat all as indicative.

## PART B - Cooling hose reference research

### B1. Does H01-H11 match the Mazda manual's hose list?

No. The Mazda 2003 RX-8 web manual names cooling hoses only generically and gives no part numbers or dimensions:

- Radiator R/I (BHE011215200W01): "Upper radiator hose", "Lower radiator hose".
- Thermostat R/I (BHE011215171W01): "Upper radiator hose", "Hose" (both on the thermostat cover side), thermostat cover, thermostat, O-ring.
- Coolant reserve tank R/I (BHE011215201W02): one "Hose".
- Engine R/I (BHE011002000W04): "radiator hose, heater hose, coolant reserve tank hose"; "AT oil cooler hose" (AT only); "Oil hose".
- Fan motor R/I (BHE011215140W01): ATF oil cooler hose (AT only).
- No throttle-body coolant hose is named in the intake-air or throttle-body pages (BHE011301005W05, BHE011320661W01). An RX8Club poster (tmak26b, 25 Sep 2009) complained the shop manual "gives you the diagram of the big hoses" only.

So H01-H11 is the site's own scheme. The catalogue names below are the only external labelling.

### B4. RHD vs LHD and variant evidence

- Atkins Rotary: FE01-61-211, FE01-61-213 and FE01-61-212D carry "Won't fit RHD cars"; F189-61-24XA (09-11) and FE01-61-24YC/YD have no RHD statement.
- RX8Club "Cooling diagram" (post 21, motodenta, 13 Feb 2021): RHD differs by heater-core side, firewall connection, "a few heater core hoses" and "water direction on toper [upper] metal pipe"; orifice presence varies by year and market. RX8Club "RHD Heater Hose" (Australia/NZ forum, Apr 2007): RHD heater hose is on the right near the washer bottle, short and horizontal with a 180-degree loop; LHD is on the left, long and sloping (one poster, not checked against a manual).
- RX-8 Owners Club UK (ChrisHolmes, 29 Sep 2021): Racing Beat fitting instructions are "for a left hooker so the heater pipe plumbing is slightly different".
- RX-8 Owners Club UK (Eddie_r32, 17 Sep 2018): of four radiator hoses in kits only three are needed as "one is for an S1 and one is for an R3" (anecdotal; Series 2 variant risk).
- Build-date splits found: hose 381C/381D and heater hose 24YC/24YD change at "VIN 041201" (production 1 Dec 2004); thermostat housing change at VIN 071119. These are US-style VIN serials from Mazdatrix/Atkins; how they map to a UK car's VIN is not established.
- No UK RHD Mazda part number for H09-H11 found in any opened source (searches of rx8ownersclub.co.uk and rx8club.com for RHD heater hose numbers returned none).
