I'm Callum, a mechatronics engineer from Scotland. I do mechanical design, and I'm happiest on projects where the mechanics, electronics and software all have to work together.

Outside work I mostly tinker with cars. I look after a Mazda RX-8, I'm a bit obsessed with rotary engines, and I wrote a Rocket League bot with another developer. The RX-8 notebook and the Rocket League notes on this site come from those.

I use SolidWorks (including FEA and CFD) and KiCad, and I write Python, C++ and firmware for ESP32 boards. The [projects page](/ProjectPages/) has the engineering work.
