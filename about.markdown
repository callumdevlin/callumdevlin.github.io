I'm Callum Devlin, a Product Design Engineer from Glasgow. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design and electronics — mechatronics projects spanning automotive components, rotary engines, phased-array antenna systems and embedded hardware.

I like projects where I can take a problem from first evidence through to a physical, tested thing: research it, design it, build the electronics for it, write the software, then put it on a bench and find out what I got wrong. My MEng project, [EnduraOMP](/ProjectPages/EnduraOMP/), is probably the best example of that approach end to end.

If you're interested in the software side of what I do, my programming projects are on [GitHub](https://github.com/callumdevlin), and you can reach me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/callumdev).

## Technical Skills

- **Mechanical design** — automotive component design, phased-array antenna mechanical design, DfM/DfA
- **CAD & simulation** — SolidWorks, finite element analysis (structural & thermal), CFD
- **Electronics** — PCB design (KiCad), embedded systems, sensor integration
- **Software** — Python and C++ development, microcontroller firmware (ESP32)
- **Prototyping & testing** — CNC/laser-cut part specification, 3D printing, bench test design and data logging
