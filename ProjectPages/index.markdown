A mix of university, industry and personal work. Each project page covers the problem, the engineering decisions behind the solution, and what was built and tested.

## Engineering Projects

[**EnduraOMP — Improving Rotary Engine Reliability**](/ProjectPages/EnduraOMP/)
My MEng individual project: an electronically controlled, closed-loop oil metering system for rotary engines, taken from baseline engine testing through to a working machined prototype.

[**MP1 — Modular Breathing Apparatus Stowage**](/ProjectPages/E1/)
A universal, crash-rated stowage system for firefighter breathing apparatus, developed alongside Emergency One. One architecture that adapts to single and twin-pack BA sets without bespoke brackets.

[**ToolSync — Digital Tracking Toolbox**](/ProjectPages/rfidtoolstorage/)
A smart toolbox that knows what's in it. RFID check-in/out, BLE tags that flash and beep when tools go missing, and a Raspberry Pi touchscreen interface — my 4th-year BEng project.
