In the Basis column, "Mazda" means the figure is in Mazda's workshop manual and "my car" means it is what I use or what is fitted. Fill quantities are starting amounts: set the final level on the dipstick or at the filler opening.

## Vehicle and drivetrain

| Item | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine | 13B-MSP Renesis, two rotor, six-port ("High power" in Mazda's tables) | Mazda |
| Gearbox | Y16M-D six-speed manual | Mazda |
| Final drive | 4.444:1 | Mazda |
| Differential | Factory "super limited-slip differential" with a low torque-bias ratio | Mazda (no figures given) |
| Wheel | 18 × 8JJ, ET50, 5 × 114.3 mm (Mazda also lists 16 × 7½JJ) | Mazda; 18 inch is what is fitted |
| Tyre | 225/45R18 91W | Mazda |
| Cold tyre pressure | Check the door placard or UK handbook. 220 kPa / 2.2 bar / 32 psi is commonly quoted | Not in Mazda's alignment pages |
| Gearbox ratios | 3.760 / 2.269 / 1.645 / 1.187 / 1.000 / 0.843; reverse 3.564 | Mazda |

## Fluids and capacities

| System | Specification | Quantity |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Engine oil, drain and refill | Mazda's European list: ACEA A1/A3, 5W-30 (or 10W-30). I run a 10W-40 semi-synthetic, which is my choice, not Mazda's | 3.3 L |
| Engine oil, with filter | As above; confirm on the dipstick | 3.5 L |
| Engine oil, after overhaul | Initial fill | 4.7 L |
| Engine oil, total dry | Twin oil cooler 6.7 L; single cooler 5.8 L. Not a routine fill | 6.7 L (twin cooler) |
| Cooling system | Ethylene-glycol coolant with soft (demineralised) water, mixed for the climate. If the cap or filler neck is marked FL22 the coolant is premixed, so do not dilute it | 9.8 L total |
| Y16M-D gearbox | SAE 75W-90, API GL-4 or GL-5 | 1.75 L |
| Differential | API GL-5, SAE 90 (Mazda). 80W-90 and 75W-90 are common equivalents but are not in Mazda's text | 1.2-1.4 L |
| Brake hydraulics | DOT 3 or DOT 4 (SAE J1703 / FMVSS 116) for the European/UK specification | Fill to the reservoir mark |
| Clutch hydraulics | DOT 3 or DOT 4, European/UK specification | Fill to the reservoir mark |
| Fuel tank | Premium unleaded. Mazda's engine specification gives no octane number (check the UK handbook); 98 RON is the figure I've worked to | 61 L approx. |

Source for capacities and fluids: Mazda [engine specifications](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE010002000T04.html), [differential oil replacement](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE031427000W02.html), [Y16M-D oil replacement](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE051127001W04.html), [brake air bleeding](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE041101021W01.html). The FL22 premix note is from Mazda bulletin TSB 01-033/07, cited second-hand; check it against the marking on your filler cap.

## Service values

| Item | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leading spark plug | Mazda's European list: NGK RE8A-L, or RE7A-L (hotter, for engines that foul plugs). My car appears to have RE7C-L, which is not in Mazda's list | Mazda / my car |
| Trailing spark plug | NGK RE9B-T | Mazda |
| Plug gap | 1.05-1.15 mm (European/UK figure; Australian cars differ). Replace beyond 1.5 mm leading or 1.4 mm trailing. Do not bend the iridium electrodes | Mazda |
| Spark-plug torque | 12.8-17.7 N·m | Mazda |
| Wheel-nut torque | 88-118 N·m, criss-cross pattern | Mazda |
| Oil pressure | 350 kPa / 3.5 bar / 50.8 psi at 3,000 rpm with the oil at 100 °C. The oil-pump relief valve opens at 441-490 kPa | Mazda |
| Fuel-line pressure | 375-450 kPa / 3.75-4.50 bar, measured with the engine stopped, ignition on and the pump run through the check connector (F/P) | Mazda |
| Fuel pressure hold | At least 200 kPa / 2.0 bar after five minutes | Mazda |
| Thermostat | Starts opening 80-84 °C; fully open at 95 °C; lift at least 8.5 mm | Mazda |
| Cooling fans, at idle, A/C off | Both low at 97 °C, back to off at 94 °C or below; both high at 101 °C, back to low at 98 °C or below | Mazda |
| Compression at 250 rpm | 830 kPa standard; 680 kPa minimum | Mazda |
| Maximum compression spread | 150 kPa between chambers of one rotor; 100 kPa between rotors | Mazda |

Source: Mazda [spark-plug inspection](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE011801009W05.html), [oil pressure inspection](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE011101003W02.html), [fuel line pressure inspection](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE011401006W05.html), [cooling fan control inspection](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE010300103W31.html), [compression inspection](https://foxed.ca/rx7manual/2003mazdarx8/esicont/en/srvc/html/BHE011002000W03.html) and the engine specifications page above.
