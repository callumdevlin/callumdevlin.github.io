Rebuild notes, clearances and rotor packing are on the [engine rebuild page](/ProjectPages/RX8/engine-rebuild/).

I take the engine out alone through the top and leave the Y16M-D gearbox in the car. Mazda's own procedure drops the engine, gearbox and crossmember out of the bottom as one unit.

Mazda's manual splits the engine into **Type A** and **Type B**. Type B is a 2009 model-year US-VIN engine with a second (upper) oil pan and a sealed oil pump. Everything else, including a 2005 UK car, is Type A.

## Before you start

- **Engine mass:** Mazda gives no figure. Published numbers run from about 112 kg (bare) to about 138 kg (dressed), so plan on 120 kg and size the crane to the top of that range, or weigh it.
- **Crane:** check its rating at full boom reach, not retracted, and use a load leveller. Put the stand beside or ahead of the car.
- **Hold-tool jobs:** the front pulley lockbolt (254-294 N·m) and the oil-pump sprocket nut need the flywheel lock. Leave the flywheel on until both are off.

| # | Remove | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bonnet, battery | Note radio/security settings, open the windows, take the bonnet off with a helper. Battery negative first, then positive. |
| 2 | Strut brace, engine cover, airbox, intake duct, airflow-meter connector, resonator/VFAD ducting | Only what blocks the crane. |
| 3 | Fuel pressure, engine oil, coolant | Depressurise the fuel system. Drain coolant through the radiator drain access panel. Recover A/C refrigerant only if the circuit must be opened. |
| 4 | Radiator, fan and shroud, upper hoses | Cap the transmission-cooler stubs on an automatic only. |
| 5 | Coolant hoses: radiator, rear heater feed/return, expansion/degas, both throttle-body hoses | Label them and tie them outside the lift path. |
| 6 | Fuel feed quick connector at the rail, then purge/EVAP and brake-booster/vacuum hoses | Cap both sides of the fuel connector. |
| 7 | Coil pack, engine earth straps | Label the four leads **L/F, T/F, L/R, T/R**. |
| 8 | Engine harness at your chosen break points | Throttle body, six injectors, four coils, eccentric-shaft, ECT and knock sensors, OMP stepper and position switch, oil-pressure switch, alternator, starter. Mazda also lists the AIR, SSV and VDI solenoids on every engine, plus the VFAD solenoid and APV motor on the high-power engine. Photograph clips and earths first. |
| 9 | Secondary-air pipe and valve connections, remaining intake/vacuum brackets, upper and lower intake manifolds | Manifolds off only if it eases the lift. Cover every open port. |
| 10 | Oxygen-sensor wiring, exhaust manifold/catalyst connection and braces | Unclip the sensor wiring from the body. Support the exhaust; never let it hang from sensor wiring. |
| 11 | A/C compressor and any belt accessories or brackets in the way | Tie the compressor to the body with its hoses still connected. The car has electric power steering, so there is no pump. |
| 12 | Oil-cooler hoses at the engine | Cap at once and expect oil. Mazda's hose joint uses a three-projection retaining clip, so plan on a new one; note banjo-bolt washer order. |
| 13 | Starter, clutch slave cylinder | Support the gearbox on a broad padded jack. Tie the slave cylinder aside without opening the line. |
| 14 | Accessible bellhousing bolts, brackets, inspection covers | Mark each bolt's length and position. The upper bolts and dowel-area brackets hide behind the engine, so check twice. |
| 15 | Engine mounts | Crane on rated lift eyes with the leveller, taking only the engine's weight. Slacken the mount fasteners, remove both mounts from the chassis, then the brackets once the engine floats. |
| 16 | Last bellhousing bolts | Adjust crane and gearbox heights until the joint opens square, then pull the engine forward until the input shaft clears the clutch. Never use wedges on the mating faces. |
| 17 | Lift out | A few centimetres at a time, changing the leveller angle. After each move check heater hoses, oil lines, rear wiring, exhaust studs, sump/crossmember and clutch/input shaft. |
| 18 | Onto the stand | Use correctly graded bolts with full thread engagement. Never rest the engine on the sump or an oil-seal face. |

The usual snags are a hidden rear earth, an upper bellhousing bolt, the oxygen-sensor loom, an oil-cooler hose, a heater hose or an A/C bracket. My first top lift took about six hours and a repeat about two; those are my times, not labour times.

Mazda's installation procedure has steps this method skips, including a PCM procedure after an engine replacement (a series of brake-pedal presses with the ignition on). Read the manual's installation page before you finish.

## On the stand

Before opening anything:

- Photograph every side of the dressed engine and mark front and rear.
- Take off the gearbox and clutch parts. Leave the flywheel on (see above).
- Strip the intake, throttle body, fuel rails and injectors, exhaust manifold, coils and leads, air-system hardware, loom and brackets.
- Blank every oil, coolant, fuel and intake opening, then degrease the outside.
- Set out separate trays for the front and rear rotors, each split into faces A, B and C. Label the stationary gears.

Never put a rotor down on an oil-seal face. Photograph spring, notch and side-piece orientation before moving it.

Keep Mazda's figures for bolt sequences and special tools open alongside; where this page and the manual differ, the manual wins.

### External parts and lubrication

| # | Remove | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Oil filter, rear and front engine hangers, ECT sensor | |
| 2 | Water-pump pulley and body, thermostat cover, thermostat | |
| 3 | Oil pan, baffle/clip, oil-level sensor, oil strainer | Don't lever on an aluminium sealing face. Type B has an upper pan too; Type A does not. |
| 4 | OMP | Keep all four pipes and nozzle positions labelled. Cap the small drillings at once. |
| 5 | Front pulley lockbolt and pulley | Lock the eccentric shaft through the flywheel first. The bolt is very tight, so do this before any shaky stand work. |
| 6 | Oil-pump bypass plug, valve and spring, then the front cover | The valve and spring are loaded. Don't drive a tool into the cover's sealing face. |
| 7 | Oil-pressure-regulator plug, spring and valve | Keep these separate from the bypass valve parts. |
| 8 | Oil-pump sprocket nut and lock washer, sprocket, chain, OMP drive gear, oil-pump gear and pump components | Mark the OMP drive-gear orientation. Flywheel still locked; unstake the nut. |
| 9 | Balance weight, thrust plate, thrust needle bearing, spacer | Keep the lettered end-float spacer identified. |
| 10 | Oil pump (inspection) | A Type A pump can be stripped for inspection; a Type B pump is sealed and replaced whole. |

### Housing stack

| # | Remove | Notes |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flywheel and counterweight | Lock with the SST, take off the locknut, and don't shock-load the eccentric shaft. |
| 2 | Tension bolts | Loosen in Mazda's figure order over two or three passes. Keep the seal washers. |
| 3 | Rear housing | Lift with a small wiggle to break the oil film. No wedges between faces. A side or corner seal may stay stuck on: put it straight back in its labelled position. |
| 4 | Rear oil seal, rear stationary gear, pressure regulator (Type A), rear dowel | |
| 5 | Rear rotor housing | Lift straight up, holding all three apex assemblies so they can't fall. Mark its orientation and the width-measurement positions. |
| 6 | Rear rotor | Per Mazda: move the side piece, apex, corner and side seals and springs from the rotor's upper face into the SST tray, break the oil film, lift the rotor, then take the seals off the lower face. Return every seal to its rear A/B/C position. |
| 7 | Front dowel, then the intermediate housing | A helper holds the eccentric shaft up about 30 mm while you lift and turn the housing just clear. Don't scrape the journal or lever on the seal track. |
| 8 | Front rotor housing | Same controlled lift. |
| 9 | Eccentric shaft, oil-jet plug, spring and ball | Keep them in order. |
| 10 | Front rotor, front plate, needle bearing, thrust plate, front stationary gear, front housing | Empty the seals into the labelled front A/B/C tray. Don't swap the stationary gears. |
