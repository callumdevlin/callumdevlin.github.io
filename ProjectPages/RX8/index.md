This is my working notebook for a 2003-2008 Series 1 RX-8, specifically a UK 231 PS six-port 13B-MSP. The notes assume that car unless a page says otherwise. They complement Mazda's workshop manual rather than replace it, and I confirm anything production-break-sensitive against the car before ordering parts.

Each page tries to keep three things apart: what Mazda publishes, what I measured on my own car, and what I've only seen in owner reports. Where something is blank, I haven't measured or verified it yet, and I've left it blank rather than guess.
