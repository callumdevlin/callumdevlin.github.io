## Jacking and support points

Mazda's manual gives two jacking points, the centre of the front crossmember and the centre of the differential, with stands on the side sills at both sides. The detail below is my own guidance from working on the car.

| Point | Where | Keep clear of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front central jack | Reinforced centre of the front suspension crossmember: the pad goes under the flat central member, behind the plastic undertray | Aluminium sump, thin undertray |
| Rear central jack | Solid centre of the differential casing | Rear cover, drain plug, cooling fins, propshaft flange, PPF |
| Axle stands (four) | Reinforced sill support areas just inboard of the pinch welds, marked by the sill notches; a slotted rubber pad protects the folded seam | |

Raise centrally, then lower onto the matching left/right pair. The notch and pinch-weld detail is from handling the car and the owner's handbook, not the workshop manual.

{% include figure.html src="/Images/RX8-JackPoints.jpg" alt="Top-down underside diagram of the RX-8 showing the two central jack points and four sill support points" caption="The two central jack points and the four sill support points, seen from underneath." %}

Never work under the car supported only by a jack. Don't load the sump, gearbox casing, PPF, propshaft, differential rear cover, floorpan or thin sheet-metal braces.

## Wheels and tyres

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Wheel | 18 × 8JJ, ET50 (Mazda also lists 16 × 7½JJ) |
| Stud pattern | 5 × 114.3 mm |
| Tyre size | 225/45R18 91W (Mazda). My fitted tyre is a Michelin Pilot Sport 5, my choice |
| Cold pressure | 220 kPa / 2.2 bar / 32 psi front and rear. Check the door placard or UK handbook; Mazda's alignment pages specify none |
| Wheel nuts | 88-118 N·m, tightened diagonally |
| Nominal tyre diameter | 659.7 mm (calculated: 2 × 0.45 × 225 mm + 18 in) |

The calculated diameter isn't the loaded rolling radius. Clean the hub and wheel seating faces, check for corrosion or cracks, and confirm the wheel sits flush before tightening. Follow the sidewall arrows on directional tyres, and keep the outside face of asymmetric tyres outwards. Directional tyres can only swap front to rear on the same side unless remounted.

## Brakes

| Item | Front | Rear |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Disc diameter | 323 mm with sport suspension (303 mm with standard suspension) | 302 mm |
| Disc thickness, new | 24 mm | 18 mm |
| Minimum thickness | 22 mm | 16 mm |
| Minimum after on-car machining | 22.8 mm | 16.8 mm |
| Maximum runout, measured 10 mm from edge | 0.05 mm | 0.05 mm |
| Maximum thickness variation | 0.015 mm | 0.015 mm |
| Minimum pad lining | 2 mm | 2 mm |
| Carrier/bracket bolts | 78.4-101.9 N·m | 49.0-68.6 N·m |
| Slider bolts | 21.6-31.4 N·m | 21.6-31.4 N·m |

- **Disc size:** Mazda ties the larger front disc to the sport suspension, not engine power, so measure yours before ordering.
- **Fluid:** DOT 3 or DOT 4 meeting SAE J1703 / FMVSS 116 (Mazda's European/UK specification). Bleed screw torque 6.9-9.8 N·m.
- **Bleeding:** Mazda starts at the master cylinder, continues with the caliper furthest from it, and finishes by bleeding the master cylinder again, keeping the reservoir at least three quarters full. It gives no wheel-by-wheel order. Mine is left rear, right rear, left front, right front on this UK RHD car, my inference from furthest to nearest, so check your pipe lengths. If the master cylinder was emptied or removed, bleed its ports first and again after the calipers.
- **Rear piston:** it incorporates the parking-brake adjuster. Wind it clockwise into the caliper with the correct tool; don't force it back with a clamp. Align the piston recess with the pad locating pin. After assembly, pump the pedal until firm before using or adjusting the handbrake. Factory lever travel is 1-3 notches under a 98 N pull measured 50 mm from the lever end.

## Alignment conditions

Check tyre pressures first. Mazda specifies a full fuel tank, correct oil and coolant, jack and tools stowed, no extra load and a settled suspension. Measure the height from the fender end to the wheel centre, because nominal camber and caster depend on ride height. Left-to-right camber and caster difference must stay within 1°.

### Toe

| Axle | Total toe at tyre | Total angle | At inner rim: standard / sport |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Front | 2 ± 4 mm | 0°11′ ± 21′ | 1.2 ± 2.5 / 1.4 ± 2.8 mm |
| Rear | 3 ± 4 mm | 0°16′ ± 20′ | 1.9 ± 2.5 / 2.1 ± 2.8 mm |

Aim for zero thrust angle by making rear individual toe equal about the vehicle centreline; Mazda gives no separate thrust-angle tolerance. Its rear alignment page prints 2 ± 4 mm in the toe-adjustment section but 3 ± 4 mm in the specification tables; I've used the tables.

### Standard-suspension camber and caster

| Front fender height | Front camber | Rear fender height | Front caster | Rear camber |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 367-376 mm | −0°33′ | 361-370 mm | 6°31′ | −1°30′ |
| 377-386 mm | −0°13′ | 371-380 mm | 6°18′ | −1°12′ |
| 387-396 mm | +0°04′ | 381-390 mm | 6°06′ | −0°56′ |
| 397-406 mm | +0°20′ | 391-400 mm | 5°53′ | −0°43′ |
| 407-416 mm | +0°33′ | 401-410 mm | 5°40′ | −0°33′ |

### Sport-suspension camber and caster

| Front fender height | Front camber | Rear fender height | Front caster | Rear camber |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 361-370 mm | −0°45′ | 354-363 mm | 6°41′ | −1°44′ |
| 371-380 mm | −0°25′ | 364-373 mm | 6°28′ | −1°24′ |
| 381-390 mm | −0°06′ | 374-383 mm | 6°16′ | −1°07′ |
| 391-400 mm | +0°11′ | 384-393 mm | 6°03′ | −0°52′ |
| 401-410 mm | +0°26′ | 394-403 mm | 5°50′ | −0°40′ |

Camber/caster tolerance is ±1° around the height-dependent nominal value in Mazda's alignment procedure; its suspension specification page lists ±45′ for the same angles, and I've used the procedure page.

Front camber/caster and rear camber eccentric nuts are 117.7-137.3 N·m. Rear toe-cam nuts are 70-95 N·m; front tie-rod locknuts are 37.0-49.0 N·m (see the [torque page](/ProjectPages/RX8/torquespecs/#steering) for Mazda's inconsistency on that one). Final-tighten fasteners through bonded-rubber bushes at normal ride height.
